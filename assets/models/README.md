# Licensed i20 models

The included scene in src/components/CarModel.tsx is an original, approximate **GB-era** procedural model with independent animated doors. It is NOT a factory-accurate scan of the 2018 car.

A free downloadable Hyundai i20 model with CC BY attribution is available from:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hyundai-i20-09f69b16a298460f96269d3074a88ea4
Author: 9810264190. License: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY).

The creator does not specify its model year, and the online download requires a Sketchfab account. We therefore do not pretend it is the 2018 GB facelift, and we do not hotlink or rehost extracted viewer assets.

For a closer 2018–20 facelift geometry, the SQUIR 2019 model is sold commercially; it is *not* a free asset. Before swapping in another model: verify body year, source and redistribution license; optimize mesh/materials and place each door under separate hinge pivots. Avoid publishing assets with personal-use-only licenses in this public repository.
