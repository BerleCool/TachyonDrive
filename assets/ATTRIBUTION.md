# Vehicle artwork

`i20-2018.png` derives from **2018 Hyundai i20 SE MPi 1.2 Front.jpg**, photographed by **Vauxford**, 16 June 2018.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2018_Hyundai_i20_SE_MPi_1.2_Front.jpg

Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/71/2018_Hyundai_i20_SE_MPi_1.2_Front.jpg

License: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes: polygon background removal, crop and resize. The derivative image is also licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. This license applies to the photograph/derivative, not to unrelated application source. No endorsement by the photographer or Hyundai is implied.

This is a real 2018 GB-generation reference photograph, not a 3D model, a VIN match, or a representation of every 2018 trim/facelift. Users may select their own photo. The earlier inaccurate procedural 3D model and pretend door controls were removed; an accurately licensed, door-rigged 2018 model has not been obtained.

`icon.png` is an original geometric Tachyon Drive mark generated by `scripts/prepare_assets.py`.

The images are bundled into the APK. The running app does not fetch them from the internet.

## Interactive 3D viewer (online only)

Android uses an official embedded Sketchfab viewer and DOES NOT ship or redistribute
the underlying car meshes. It requires internet and may set third-party cookies.

- User-selected **Hyundai i20 Active 2019** model: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hyundai-i20-actilve-2019-c1ae84af72d44b9ebda34b718aafbbf2
  A similarly named high-detail SQUIR model is commercially sold; it is NOT treated as free to download or redistribute.
- Alternate free-view **hyundai_i20** uploaded by 9810264190:
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hyundai-i20-09f69b16a298460f96269d3074a88ea4
  This is listed as downloadable under CC BY, but its exact generation is not confirmed.

The online embed is view/rotate/zoom only. It is not a bundled offline GLB or a door-rigged model; no physical car controls are available.
